Veterans fund

The aim is simple: help veterans, including people who served and do not have a home. The method is a public address you can watch — not a hidden pile of coins, and not a cut taken from every payment.

Official address

Not published yet

It goes live on this page on public launch day (height 0). Until then there is nothing to send to.

How money gets here

  • Anyone can send RUCK to the published address after launch.
  • The official wallet will have a line at send time: “Add a donation for veterans.” It stays off unless you turn it on. You pick the amount.
  • Other wallets do not have to offer that line. We will not claim “every transaction” pays the fund.

How money leaves

This page will list outflows: date, amount, who was helped or which organization, and the transaction id. If we cannot show that, we are failing the promise.

What we will not do

  • No premine for this fund.
  • No automatic cut of miner fees or of the 5,000 RUCK block reward.
  • No lock until a price, a market cap, or “the coin grows.” Help should not wait on a market. After launch, coins here are for documented aid, not for sitting until a chart looks good.
  • No address in a private message. If it is not on this page, it is not us.

Today. Private test only. The public network is not open. Anyone collecting “RuckCoin for veterans” right now is not this project.
