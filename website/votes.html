Holders decide unlocks

Votes

After official launch, locked ops and listing coins do not move because we feel like it. A proposal goes on this page. People using the official wallet, who hold at least the published minimum of RUCK, vote yes or no. If the vote fails, the coins stay locked.

Who can vote

Official wallet + enough RUCK

  1. Use the official wallet (the one from this site).
  2. Hold at least the minimum on a K address you control. That minimum is printed here at height 0.
  3. Your vote is tied to that address — not to an email, and not to a click we can fake.

Minimum to vote: not set yet (printed at official launch).

What a vote can do

Unlock a named amount

Each proposal is one job: which lock, how much, why (ops bill, named exchange deposit, named liquidity). Not a blank check. Not the veterans fund — that pot is aid, not a vote prize.

How it is counted

Weight is coins, record is public

One RUCK, one weight, at a snapshot height printed on the proposal. Yes must beat no, and yes must clear a quorum printed at launch. The site shows the race. The spend still needs a txid on the locks table that matches the winning proposal.

How you will vote (after launch)

  1. Read the proposal on this page

    Amount, lock, reason, start height, end height, snapshot height. If those fields are missing, it is not a real proposal.

  2. Vote from the official wallet

    The wallet will offer Yes / No on an open proposal. Under the hood that is a signed message from your K address, or a Ravencoin-style vote asset sent to a tally address — we will pick one method and print it here before the first vote. The site is the bulletin board. The keys stay on your computer.

  3. Result, then the spend

    When the window closes we publish yes weight, no weight, and pass/fail. A pass still has to show up as a matching outflow on the locks page. A fail means nothing moves.

Rules that keep this from being theater

  • We cannot pass a proposal by clicking “yes” on the server. Weight comes from addresses that hold RUCK.
  • We cannot spend a lock without a passed proposal that matches the amount and the reason.
  • Test-chain coins and test-chain votes do not count.
  • Buying a vote in a chat is still just a chat. The chain and the snapshot are what count.
  • If the official wallet and this page ever disagree on a result, the published signed votes / on-chain tally wins — not a screenshot.
