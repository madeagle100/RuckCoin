For your computer

The wallet

This is the official RuckCoin wallet. It is built for people who have never used a coin before. Your address is on the first screen. Receive, send, and activity are in plain words. Mining is optional and explained before you click anything.

What you need. A RuckCoin node running on the same computer (the program that keeps the notebook). The wallet is the friendly window. The node is the engine. The wallet never phones home. In Settings you can also tell the node to stay offline so it talks to no other computers. After public launch we will ship a one-click pack. Today: start the node, then open the wallet.

After you download

  1. Unzip the folder

    Keep the files together. Prefer Start RuckCoin.bat (node + wallet). Or double-click Open Wallet.bat if the node is already running.

  2. Your browser opens to your address

    That long K… string is your mailbox. Copy it when someone should pay you. Never type it from memory.

  3. Skip Mine until you are curious

    You do not need to mine to hold or send RUCK. Mining is how new coins are created. The Mine tab explains it first.
