What the numbers mean

You can spend is money that is fully yours now. Still waiting is usually coins from a brand-new mined block — they unlock after more blocks are added. That is normal, not a bug.

  1. Receive Copy your address and give it to someone (or to yourself as a test).
  2. Send Pay a K address. Sends cannot be undone.
  3. Mine Optional. Creates new coins if you find a block. You do not need to mine to use this wallet.

Mining, in plain words

Mining is how new RUCK is created. Computers guess at a hard puzzle. The one that finds the answer writes the next page of the notebook and is paid 5,000 RUCK. You do not need to mine to receive, hold, or send coins.

First time? Skip this tab until you have received or sent once. Come back when you want to try creating a coin yourself.
