What the numbers mean

You can spend is money that is fully yours now. Still waiting is usually coins from a brand-new mined block — they unlock after more blocks are added. That is normal, not a bug.

  1. Receive Copy your address and give it to someone (or to yourself as a test).
  2. Send Pay a K address. Sends cannot be undone.
  3. Look up Paste a block number, a payment id, or a K address to see what the notebook says.
  4. Mine Optional. Creates new coins if you find a block. You do not need to mine to use this wallet.

Pay someone

Paste the other person’s K address and an amount. There is no undo and no bank to call.

Mining, in plain words

Mining is how new RUCK is created. Computers guess at a hard puzzle. The one that finds the answer writes the next page of the notebook and is paid 5,000 RUCK. You do not need to mine to receive, hold, or send coins.

First time? Skip this tab until you have received or sent once. Come back when you want to try creating a coin yourself.

This computer

Settings

The wallet does not store the whole chain. It asks your local node. These boxes tell it where that node is. Leave them as they are if you used the RuckCoin desktop starters.

Stay off the internet

This wallet never sends your keys or balance to our servers. It only talks to the node on this computer. If you also want that node to stop talking to other people’s computers, turn this on.

Works without internet: see your address, see the last balance this computer knows, make a new address, prepare a send.

Needs a connection later: actually hear about new incoming payments, and have a send show up for the other person. Those wait until you turn the internet back on.
